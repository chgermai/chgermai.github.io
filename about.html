What Retro Gaming Means to Me

Retro gaming is a mix of creativity and problem solving. Classic systems limit what developers were able to do. Instead of having on big gaming engines or unlimited assets, as are available in today's systems, the focus was on the gameplay, controls, pacing, and feedback.

How Hardware Constraints Improved Design

  • Memory limits encouragd small, reusable patterns.
  • Visual limits pushed developers towards readable and iconic shapes.
  • Control limits focus the games on one or two strong mechanics.
  • Performance limits reward efficientancy and iteration.

What This Project Demonstrates

This website is intentionally simple: two pages, consistent styling, and clear navigation. The goal is to show a prototype project that practices the process of scoping, planning, risk analysis, and reflection using a professional GitHub repository and a live GitHub Pages deployment.
